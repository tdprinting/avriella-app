No Wi-Fi? Turn on Personal Hotspot on this iPhone, join it from her phone once (it remembers), and open Avriella TV. Her phone then shows a 172.20.10.x address. Type it here.

Tip: on her phone, set a fixed IP of 172.20.10.9 for your hotspot (Wi-Fi → your hotspot → Edit → IP settings: Static, gateway 172.20.10.1) so the address never changes.
