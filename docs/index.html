Add a Tapo camera

First time? Turn on the camera's video link
  1. Open the Tapo app and tap the camera.
  2. Tap the gear (top right) → Advanced Settings → Camera Account.
  3. Make a username and password (letters and numbers are easiest). You can use the same one on every camera.
  4. Come back here, tap Find cameras, pick it and type that username and password.

The camera's login is kept on her phone and only used to open the camera's video on your Wi-Fi.
